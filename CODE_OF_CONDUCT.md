# Code of Conduct

LiteDisplay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or unwelcome personal attention, in any language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space related to the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes participation difficult for you or others, report it privately to the maintainer
through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/renatoasterio). Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block a contributor for behaviour considered inappropriate, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
